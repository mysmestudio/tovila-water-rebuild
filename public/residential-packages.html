Residential Water Packages

Most Ghanaian households don't need a single isolated filter. They require a cohesive, engineered system that coordinates filtration, softening, drinking purification, and disinfection around their specific water source and family size.

Engineering Logic

Why Engineered Packages?

Water quality challenges in Ghana rarely happen in isolation. A home with hard borehole water often also suffers from fine silt, seasonal coliform bacteria, and high total dissolved solids.

Purchasing individual filters piecemeal usually results in mismatched flow rates, premature cartridge blinding, pressure drops, and wasted expenditure.

Tovila's residential packages are systematically balanced. Every stage operates within calibrated hydraulic flow tolerances: pre-filtration shields softening resin, soft water protects RO membranes, and sterile UV light polishes clear water without chemical by-products.

Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I customise a package to fit my specific home?

Absolutely. Our five tiers serve as established engineering baselines, but every Tovila installation is customised. If you select the Premium tier but also want an under-sink RO system, or require custom iron-removal media for an aggressive borehole, we modify the skid layout accordingly.

What is the typical installation timeline?

Typical · Installation completed in 1 to 2 business days

Once an on-site water assessment is conducted and your customized package is confirmed, our engineering crew pre-assembles and pressure-tests your skid offsite. On-site plumbing installation and commissioning typically takes between 1 and 2 business days with minimal disruption to your daily routine.

Is a water assessment strictly required first?

Yes. We strongly discourage blind installations. Installing a water softener on water with undetected high iron will foul the resin, just as installing UV on turbid water will fail to kill bacteria. A laboratory or on-site water test guarantees that the package you select will perform as promised.

What happens after installation?

Every package includes Tovila's post-installation handover: water testing to verify treatment efficacy, homeowner system orientation, and enrollment in our preventive maintenance schedule with scheduled filter cartridge and salt deliveries.
