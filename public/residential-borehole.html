Borehole Water Treatment

Every borehole in Ghana has its own geological fingerprint. From heavy dissolved iron and manganese to calcium hardness and coliform bacteria, our multi-stage engineering transforms raw groundwater into crystal-clear domestic water.

Groundwater Geology

How Borehole Water Treatment Works

Unlike municipal surface water that has undergone municipal sedimentation, borehole water originates deep underground in oxygen-depleted bedrock aquifers.

In this anaerobic environment, minerals like iron (Fe2+) and manganese (Mn2+) exist dissolved in a completely clear, soluble state. However, the instant your submersible pump lifts this water to your surface tanks and exposes it to atmospheric oxygen, these minerals oxidize into insoluble red-orange rust flakes and black manganese sludge.

Because no single catalogue filter can resolve every borehole complication, treatment is strictly source-specific. Tovila designs a treatment train combining oxidation, catalytic media filtration, ion-exchange softening, and UV disinfection, precisely matched to your water's laboratory chemical analysis.

Engineering Philosophy

Test Before You Treat

Installing off-the-shelf cartridge filters on raw borehole water often leads to clogged cartridges within days, burnt-out pump motors, and persistent wall staining.

Tovila begins with a comprehensive physical, chemical, and biological lab assessment, ensuring you only invest in equipment that addresses your borehole's specific mineral profile.

Symptom Checklist

Signs Your Borehole Needs Treatment

Raw groundwater manifests its chemical load through visible damage across your home:

⚒

Orange or Red Iron Staining

Rusty red or brown staining appearing on bathroom tiles, toilet bowls, sinks, and ruining light-coloured laundry.

☼

Black Specks & Manganese

Dark, oily or black grit settling in water cisterns and shower floors, accompanied by a harsh metallic aftertaste.

❄

Rapid Scale & Heavy Hardness

Severe limescale encrusting water heater elements, blocking overhead showerheads, and leaving thick white scum rings.

⚙

Cloudy Silt & Rotten Egg Odour

Fine rock flour or sand wearing out plumbing valves, or pungent hydrogen sulphide gas releasing when hot water runs.

Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

My borehole water looks completely clear—do I still need treatment?

Yes, absolutely. Appearance is deceptive with groundwater. Dissolved ferrous iron and manganese remain 100% invisible and clear until exposed to air inside your home's storage tanks or piping. Furthermore, dangerous bacterial pathogens, heavy metals, and high mineral hardness are completely colourless. Professional water testing is the only way to confirm true safety.

Can borehole water quality change between dry and rainy seasons?

Yes. During the rainy season, heavy surface recharge can wash agricultural fertilisers, septic leachate, and microbial coliforms into shallow or fractured bedrock aquifers. In the dry season, receding water tables often concentrate dissolved mineral hardness and iron. Tovila designs systems robust enough to handle these seasonal swings.

Can I drink treated borehole water directly?

Once treated through our recommended multi-barrier train—incorporating pre-filtration, iron/manganese removal, softening, and UV disinfection—your utility water is clean and safe. For kitchen culinary and drinking water, we strongly advocate adding a dedicated Home Reverse Osmosis unit to eliminate all residual dissolved solids and guarantee optimal taste.

How long does a borehole water treatment system last?

Typical · Automatic backwash media lifespan: 5 to 8+ years

Our commercial-grade fiberglass pressure vessels and automated digital backwash valves are engineered for long service life. The catalytic media beds regenerate automatically via water backwash, delivering 5 to 8+ years of reliable service before media replacement is required.
