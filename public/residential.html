Better Water Starts at Home.

Before any product: where does your water come from? Borehole, municipal mains, a stored tank or a mix of all three — each behaves differently through the year, and the right system is the one designed for your actual source.

Source first

Four supplies, four different problems

Borehole

Often hard, sometimes high in iron or manganese, and quality shifts with the seasons.

Municipal

Treated at source but arrives with chlorine taste, pressure swings and pipe-borne sediment.

Stored / tanker

Storage is where biological growth and settled sediment start, whatever the source was.

Mixed supply

Switching between sources means treatment must cope with the worst of both.
