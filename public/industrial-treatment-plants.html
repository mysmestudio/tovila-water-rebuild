Water Treatment Plants

Project Delivery Model

Eliminating Handover Friction Through Single-Source Accountability

Industrial water treatment installations frequently fail due to fragmentation. When process designers, skid fabricators, mechanical pipefitters, and electrical contractors operate independently, accountability dissolves. Minor interface misalignments lead to unexpected delays, unbudgeted site modifications, and disputes over performance guarantees when the plant is brought online.

Tovila manages the complete lifecycle from process simulation and mechanical layout to fabrication oversight and final site commissioning. By maintaining continuous engineering oversight through every phase, we verify weld integrity, pressure ratings, pipe slope, and control logic before components arrive on site, ensuring seamless integration into active industrial facilities.

Engineering Oversight

Quality Verification Before Site Arrival

Fabrication oversight ensures every pressure vessel, pump skid frame, valve manifold, and control enclosure is inspected and hydrostatically tested before dispatch. This pre-delivery verification dramatically shortens on-site installation timelines and eliminates costly rework.

Note: Plant dimensions, foundation pad load capacities, pipe diameters, and electrical power demands are determined during the engineering assessment for that site.

Lifecycle Workflow

Four Stages of Plant Execution

A disciplined engineering methodology delivering predictable performance from concept to handover.

1. Front-End Engineering Design (FEED)

Detailed process flow diagrams (PFDs), mass and energy balances, piping and instrumentation diagrams (P&IDs), and complete equipment schedules developed from source water chemistry and process demand curves.

  • Comprehensive raw water characterization and seasonal variability analysis
  • Process modeling and hydraulic head-loss calculations
  • Civil interface specifications and structural pad load requirements

2. Fabrication Oversight & Quality Control

Strict manufacturing surveillance covering skid framing metallurgy, vessel weld inspections, internal lining and coating thickness verification, and hydrostatic pressure testing prior to site delivery.

  • Non-destructive weld testing and dimensional skid tolerance checks
  • Protective coating and passivation verification on stainless steel assemblies
  • Pre-shipment hydrostatic pressure testing and factory witness inspections

3. Mechanical & Electrical Installation

Precision on-site installation executed by trained mechanical and electrical specialists. Covers structural skid mounting, interconnecting process piping, valve manifolds, cable tray routing, and control panel wiring.

  • Rigid pipe support alignment to prevent vibration and hydraulic shock
  • Segregated power, control, and instrumentation cabling
  • Integration into facility raw water intake and treated water distribution manifolds

4. Commissioning & Performance Handover

Systematic cold and hot commissioning protocol verifying instrumentation loop calibration, automated valve sequencing, failsafe shutoffs, chemical dosing accuracy, and water quality compliance against basis-of-design targets.

  • Full loop testing and safety interlock verification
  • Treated water parameter validation across varied operating load cycles
  • Formal plant handover dossier with as-built drawings and maintenance procedures
Operational Continuity

Engineered for Serviceability & Plant Longevity

Tovila designs water treatment plants with maintenance access as an explicit design criterion. Adequate valve clearances, instrument bypass manifolds, and modular skid layouts ensure that routine cartridge replacements, pump servicing, or sensor recalibrations can be performed efficiently without shutting down entire production lines.

Site Specifics

Treatment stages, vessel metallurgy, chemical injection skids, and automation architecture are tailored during the engineering assessment for that site.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Is fabrication done on-site or off-site?

Fabrication methodology depends on site access, footprint constraints, and project scope. Wherever feasible, modular equipment skids and pre-wired control panels are prefabricated and tested in controlled workshop conditions off-site to reduce operational disruption during on-site installation.

What does commissioning include?

Commissioning includes comprehensive loop checking, sensor recalibration, hydraulic stress testing, chemical dosing verification, automated fail-safe testing, and continuous water quality sampling to verify performance against the basis-of-design documentation prior to commercial handover.
