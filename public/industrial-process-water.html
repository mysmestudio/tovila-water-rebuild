Process Water Engineering

Water specified to the exact chemical and physical tolerances of your production line, preventing batch contamination, equipment scaling, and unscheduled plant downtime.

Talk to our engineering team Explore Process Tolerances
Engineering Philosophy

Designing Backward from Process Chemistry

Standard municipal or potable water standards are built around human health guidelines. However, industrial manufacturing equipment and chemical processes possess entirely different sensitivities. Water that is perfectly safe to drink can destroy a high-pressure steam boiler, spoil a beverage formulation, corrode cooling loops, or ruin an electro-deposition bath.

Tovila engineers process water backward from the receiving equipment's operational envelope. By evaluating OEM technical manuals, chemical solubility equilibria, and product purity requirements, we establish strict limit envelopes for silica, conductivity, hardness, dissolved gases, and organic carbon.

The Specification Imperative

Tolerances, Not Generalities

Whether mitigating refractory silica scaling in thermal cycles or controlling ionic conductivity in high-precision manufacturing, our treatment trains isolate and remove the specific chemical species that threaten production continuity.

Note: Conductivity limits, silica thresholds, dissolved oxygen ppm, and pH operating bounds are defined from equipment manufacturer specifications and production chemistry requirements.

Targeted Applications

Critical Industrial Process Demands

Different production lines demand unique purification trains. We engineer specific barriers for each:

Boiler Feed & Steam Generation

Steam boilers operate under extreme temperature and pressure where dissolved minerals rapidly deposit as insulating scale, triggering tube overheating, corrosion, and catastrophic failure.

  • Deep demineralization targeting silica, calcium, and magnesium elimination
  • Thermal or catalytic deaeration to eliminate dissolved oxygen and carbon dioxide
  • Continuous blowdown minimization through precise feed water conductivity control

Food & Beverage Formulation

Product water becomes an ingredient in the finished good. Trace ions, chlorine residuals, and alkalinity imbalances alter flavor profiles, beverage clarity, carbonation stability, and shelf life.

  • Total organic carbon (TOC) and volatile aromatic compound stripping
  • Alkalinity correction and balanced remineralization manifolds
  • Absolute sub-micron filtration paired with sanitary ultraviolet disinfection

Pharmaceutical & Cleanroom Utilities

High-purity rinse water, compounding utilities, and equipment washdown require tight microbial control, low conductivity, and complete endotoxin exclusion.

  • Sanitary orbital-welded stainless steel distribution loops with velocity control
  • Continuous electrolytic deionization (EDI) polishing
  • Ozone sanitization loops with automated ultraviolet destruct units

Cooling Towers & Closed Loops

Cooling circuits suffer from evaporative mineral concentration, microbiological slime growth, and micro-corrosion that degrade heat exchanger heat transfer efficiency.

  • Automated bleed and makeup water conductivity equalization
  • Inline side-stream centrifugal and sand filtration
  • Targeted biocidal dosing and corrosion inhibitor proportional control
Technical Contrast

Why Potable Standards Fall Short in Manufacturing

Drinking water regulations allow moderate levels of calcium, magnesium, sulfates, and chlorides that are healthy for human consumption but ruin industrial operations. Installing off-the-shelf commercial filters without calculating the Langelier Saturation Index (LSI) or Ryznar Stability Index (RSI) results in hidden fouling that impairs thermal transfer efficiency and elevates energy draw across process equipment.

Site Specifics

Treatment stages, deionization resins, membrane train configurations, and degassing equipment are selected during the engineering assessment for that site.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from general water treatment?

A generic treatment approach targets broad drinking water standards suitable for human ingestion. Process water engineering works backward from the process's actual chemical and physical tolerance requirements — such as zero silica for boilers or controlled conductivity for precision manufacturing.

What industries typically need this?

Any process-sensitive manufacturing operation — food and beverage bottling, pharmaceuticals, cosmetics, chemical synthesis, thermal power plants, packaging, and commercial laundries — where water composition directly impacts equipment integrity or final product yield.
