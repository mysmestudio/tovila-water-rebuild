Facilities Management

Fewer Maintenance Calls, Lower Overhead, Satisfied Tenants

In corporate office towers, business parks, and multi-tenant commercial complexes, water management is primarily an operational efficiency challenge. Managing hundreds of bulky 20-litre plastic water bottles clutters hallways, consumes storage space, and creates administrative friction. Meanwhile, untreated water running through building rest rooms leads to stained fixtures, recurring complaints, and constant plumbing maintenance calls.

Equally critical is building mechanical infrastructure. Central air conditioning systems, chillers, and evaporative cooling towers circulate thousands of litres of water daily. When make-up water carries unconditioned minerals and silica, scale crystallises on condenser tubes, causing heat transfer efficiency to plummet and triggering expensive maintenance downtime. Tovila delivers comprehensive water engineering that protects both building assets and tenant workplace comfort.

Operational Focus

Protecting Mechanical Assets & Daily Comfort

By conditioning raw incoming supply at the building's central utility plant, we provide treated make-up water that stops cooling tower scaling and pipe corrosion while supplying multi-floor plumbed drinking stations with pure, taste-free water that eliminates reliance on bottled deliveries.

Note: Sizing for cooling tower make-up, drinking dispenser networks, and central filtration is confirmed per facility based on building occupancy and HVAC engineering specifications.

Office Scope

Four Pillars of Workplace Water Engineering

Tovila addresses workplace drinking amenities, facility operations, and central HVAC protection through integrated water engineering.

Safe Drinking Points Across Floors

Directly plumbed, multi-stage purified drinking water points for office kitchenettes, executive suites, and cafeteria zones. Our systems eliminate the logistics, security passes, and heavy lifting of 20-litre bottles, delivering an unlimited, fresh supply of pristine chilled and ambient drinking water to staff and visitors.

  • Replaces bottle deliveries with plumbed, bottleless purification
  • Provides multi-barrier sub-micron filtration and UV disinfection
  • Delivers consistent, refreshing taste without chemical odours

Low-Maintenance Dispensers

Heavy-duty filtration and inline sediment defense protecting chilled, ambient, and boiling water dispensers from clogging and valve calcification. By preventing mineral buildup in internal heating blocks and cooling coils, we extend dispenser service intervals and reduce routine technician call-outs.

  • Prevents mineral clogging in boiling water solenoid valves
  • Long-life filter configurations minimize service disruption
  • Hygienic touchless and lever dispense options available

Cooling & HVAC Make-Up Water

Targeted softening, demineralisation, and corrosion inhibition for central chiller plants and evaporative cooling towers. Conditioning raw make-up water prevents scale formation on condenser heat exchanger tubes, protects cooling tower fill packs, and mitigates biological fouling across the building's central HVAC network.

  • Prevents insulating mineral scale on central chiller tubes
  • Reduces cooling tower bleed-off frequency and water waste
  • Inhibits internal pipe corrosion throughout closed cooling loops

Tenant Complaint Reduction

Whole-building conditioning eliminates seasonal rust discolouration, chlorine fumes, and mineral grit from restroom basins and toilets. By maintaining steady pressure and clean, clear water throughout vertical risers, facilities teams resolve the root cause of frequent tenant maintenance tickets.

  • Stops porcelain staining and sediment buildup in flush valves
  • Maintains consistent hydraulic pressure across upper and lower floors
  • Eliminates persistent water quality complaints from tenant teams
Facility Maintenance

Comprehensive Preventative Maintenance Agreements

Commercial facilities teams need predictable maintenance schedules, not reactive emergencies. Tovila provides scheduled quarterly inspections, consumable filter replacements, and chiller loop water testing under structured service level agreements, ensuring office water systems operate smoothly without burdening internal property staff.

Site Specifics

Specific dispenser counts, cooling tower chemical dosing parameters, and central pump skid capacities are confirmed per site based on architectural plans, occupant counts, and mechanical load surveys.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this cover HVAC cooling towers too?

Yes. Our commercial engineering scope covers both potable drinking systems and mechanical make-up water treatment. Conditioning the make-up water feeding cooling towers and central HVAC loops prevents scale formation on condenser tubes and protects building mechanical assets from costly corrosion.

How does this reduce tenant complaints?

Consistent whole-building filtration removes seasonal sediment, rust particles, and chlorine odours at the point of entry. Delivering crystal-clear, reliable water to restrooms and pantries eliminates the daily taste, smell, and staining issues that prompt occupants to submit facilities tickets.
