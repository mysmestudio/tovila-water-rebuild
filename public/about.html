About

Built in Ghana, Engineering for a Continent.

Tovila Water Solutions exists because water in this region is too variable to be solved with imported catalogues and guesswork.

Our story

From Tetegu outward

We started in Tetegu, Accra, working on boreholes and household systems where the water changed character between the dry and rainy seasons and no two sites behaved alike. That taught us the discipline the company now runs on: test first, design to the source, and never sell a system you cannot service.

From homes we moved into hotels, estates, schools and clinics, then into industrial treatment, wastewater and automation. The ambition is pan-African — the standard is set here, on Ghanaian water, with Ghanaian technicians we train ourselves.
