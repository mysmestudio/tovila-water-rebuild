Designed for Working Professionals

Flexible, Practical Engineering Education

Facility managers, hotel maintenance engineers, estate supervisors, and plant technicians have demanding, shift-based work schedules. Our online curriculum is built from the ground up for asynchronous study.

Zero Downtime

Why Self-Paced Fits Work

Full-time employment cannot be paused for traditional weekday lectures. With modular, self-paced access, you progress during evening shifts, weekend breaks, or quiet plant hours.

All video lectures, engineering calculators, P&ID schematics, and reading notes remain accessible on mobile devices and desktops, allowing you to review specific formulas right from your plant room.

Empirical Science

Rooted in Ghanaian Water Conditions

International textbooks frequently assume clean municipal tap water as their baseline. Tovila’s courses address the actual chemical and physical conditions encountered across West Africa.

You will analyze real laboratory assays showing coastal saline intrusion, high dissolved iron, colloidal clay turbidity, manganese staining, and microbial contamination common to regional boreholes.

Immediate Utility

Every module directly translates into plant actions. Learn to calculate antiscalant dosing rates in the evening; inspect your facility’s chemical metering stroke length the next morning.

Understand how differential pressure dictates filter backwash intervals so you never backwash on guesswork again.

Core Curriculum

Three Comprehensive Topic Areas

The course modules cover the complete arc of modern water treatment — from raw sample chemistry to multi-barrier treatment configurations and daily plant stewardship.

Topic Area 01

Water Chemistry & Analysis

Mastering the science behind the sample:

  • Lab Report Interpretation: Reading physical, chemical, and microbiological water quality assays against WHO and Ghana Standards Authority (GSA) thresholds.
  • Mineral Balance: Dissolved salts, Total Dissolved Solids (TDS), electrical conductivity, calcium/magnesium hardness, and alkalinity.
  • Oxidation Dynamics: Speciation of iron (ferrous vs. ferric) and manganese, aeration kinetics, and chlorination stoichiometry.
  • Scaling & Fouling Indices: Langelier Saturation Index (LSI), Ryznar Stability Index (RSI), and Silt Density Index (SDI).
Topic Area 02

Treatment Stages & Unit Operations

Engineering the multi-barrier purification sequence:

  • Primary Clarification: Coagulation with alum and polyaluminium chloride (PAC), flocculation mechanics, and settling basins.
  • Media Filtration: Sizing sand, gravel, and anthracite layers; calculating hydraulic loading rates; and determining backwash velocities.
  • Adsorption & Softening: Virgin granular activated carbon (GAC) for chlorine/odour, and strong acid cation resin regeneration.
  • Membrane Separation: Microfiltration, ultrafiltration, and reverse osmosis membrane transport physics.
Topic Area 03

System Operation & Stewardship

Running plants reliably with zero unplanned downtime:

  • Instrumentation & Metrics: Pressure gauge calibration, rotameter reading, differential pressure tracking, and flow balancing.
  • Chemical Dosing Control: Calculating hypochlorite sanitization concentrations, antiscalant thresholds, and sodium metabisulfite neutralization.
  • Sanitary Protocols: Chemical cleaning (CIP) chemistry, periodic tank disinfections, and pipeline swabbing regimes.
  • Preventive Maintenance: Pump seal inspection, valve lubrication, filter bed inspection, and logging procedures.

Enrolment Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential details regarding the Online Courses track.

Is there a certificate on completion?

Yes. Participants who finish all module assessments, review quizzes, and final case studies receive a formal certificate of completion from Tovila Water Engineering Academy. The exact certification format, digital credential, and transcript details are confirmed at enrollment.

How long does it typically take?

Because this programme is entirely self-paced, the duration varies depending on your weekly hours of study and existing background in science or facilities management. Typical completion timeframes and recommended study tracks are confirmed at enrollment.

Is this purely theoretical?

Yes, this track serves as the theoretical and mathematical foundation. It equips you with the scientific understanding of why water behaves the way it does. For participants seeking physical pipe assembly, pump wiring, and workshop skid commissioning, it pairs directly with our Hands-On RO Training.
